FAQ

Questions, answered.

Common questions about web development with Nov8rs (Innovators).

What web development services does Nov8rs offer?+

Nov8rs provides end-to-end web development services for businesses that need a site that looks great and drives enquiries. We deliver custom website development, website design and development, landing page development services, sales funnel development, and platform builds in GoHighLevel and ClickFunnels. We can also connect forms, email/SMS follow-up and reporting so your website and automation work together, not as disconnected tools.

How much does website development cost?+

Website development costs depend on scope: page count, custom design, integrations, copy, SEO requirements, and whether you need funnels and automation. As a website development company, Nov8rs typically quotes after a short discovery so we can recommend the right build (for example a conversion-focused one-page site vs a multi-page business website development project). You’ll get a clear breakdown of deliverables, timeline and optional add-ons.

Can you build a custom website for my business?+

Yes. We specialise in custom web development and custom website development services built around your offer, brand, and sales process. That includes information architecture, conversion-focused web design, and the technical build (speed, responsiveness and tracking). Whether you need a service-based site, a lead generation website development build, or a platform-led experience, we’ll recommend a structure that makes it easy for prospects to take the next step.

Do you provide landing page development and design?+

We do. Our landing page development services include landing page design, copy structure, form setup, tracking, and A/B-ready layouts. If you need custom landing page development for a campaign or paid ads, we focus on clarity and conversion landing page design: one message, one offer, one primary action. We can also support landing page optimisation after launch by improving speed, structure and drop-off points.

Can you build sales funnels?+

Yes—Nov8rs offers funnel development services for lead generation funnels, booked calls and offer-led funnels. A typical sales funnel development project can include a lead generation landing page, a thank-you/booking step, email or SMS follow-up, and reporting. As a sales funnel development agency, we design flows around your buyer journey and remove friction, then iterate with sales funnel optimisation once you have real traffic and conversion data.

Do you provide GoHighLevel website and funnel development?+

Yes. Our GoHighLevel services cover GoHighLevel website development, GoHighLevel funnel development, and GoHighLevel landing page builds. We’ll map the pages you need, build them inside GoHighLevel, and ensure forms, calendars and pipelines are connected properly. If you’re looking for a GoHighLevel agency to replace a patchwork of tools with one platform, we can recommend the right structure and implement it cleanly.

Can you set up GoHighLevel automation and workflows?+

We can. GoHighLevel setup services often include pipeline stages, tags, forms, calendars, templates, and GoHighLevel workflow automation for lead follow up automation and sales automation services. We keep automations practical: what happens when a lead opts in, books, no-shows, or requests a quote. Where needed, we also implement GoHighLevel integrations (for example Stripe, Google Sheets or Zapier) to match your operations.

Do you provide ClickFunnels development and setup?+

Yes—Nov8rs provides ClickFunnels services including ClickFunnels funnel development, ClickFunnels funnel design, and ClickFunnels landing page builds. If you need ClickFunnels setup for a specific offer, we’ll create the pages, connect forms and follow-up, and ensure tracking is in place. As a ClickFunnels agency partner, we can also tidy up existing funnels and improve the conversion flow without rebuilding everything.

Can you connect my website, CRM, funnels and marketing automation?+

Yes. We provide marketing automation services that connect your website, CRM, funnels and follow-up so leads don’t slip through the cracks. That may include form-to-CRM routing, pipeline updates, lead generation automation, appointment reminders, and workflow automation services across email and SMS. We’ll also document the flow so your team understands what happens at each step and can maintain it over time.

Do you provide SEO-friendly website development?+

Yes. Our SEO-friendly website development approach focuses on clean structure, fast performance, mobile usability, and content that matches real search intent. As part of our professional web development services, we build sensible page hierarchies, internal linking, and metadata foundations so your site is ready for ongoing SEO. If you already have keyword priorities, we can plan pages to support them without keyword stuffing.

Do you offer white-label web development for agencies?+

Yes—Nov8rs provides white label web development services for agencies that want reliable delivery without hiring in-house. This can include white label website development, white label landing page development, and white label funnel development, plus white label GoHighLevel services where needed. We can work to your SOPs and brand guidelines, with clear communication and handover notes so you stay in control of the client relationship.

Why choose a web development agency instead of a freelancer?+

A web development agency typically brings a repeatable process: strategy, design, development, QA, and support—plus experience integrating tools like GoHighLevel and ClickFunnels. With a freelancer, you may get great work, but capacity and continuity can be a risk. As a web development company, Nov8rs can cover web design and development services and marketing automation agency work as one coordinated build.
