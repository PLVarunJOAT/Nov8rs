FAQ

Questions, answered.

Common questions about paid media with Nov8rs (Innovators).

What does a paid media agency do?+

A paid media agency plans, launches and manages paid advertising, then optimises it to improve results over time. Nov8rs operates as a paid media agency and performance marketing agency: we handle strategy, audience and keyword research, campaign setup, ad copy and creative testing, conversion tracking, budget management, and continuous optimisation. The focus is on measurable outcomes—higher-quality leads or sales, lower wasted spend, and clearer ROI/ROAS reporting.

Which paid media services does Nov8rs manage in the USA?+

As a digital advertising agency, Nov8rs manages paid media services across Google Ads (Google search ads and other Google Ads formats), Microsoft Advertising (Bing), LinkedIn Ads, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram advertising). We also support performance marketing services like tracking, landing page optimisation, and testing frameworks—so the channel mix, measurement and budgets align to one strategy rather than disconnected campaigns.

Do you build new campaigns or only optimise existing ad accounts?+

Both. Nov8rs builds, launches, manages and continuously optimises advertising campaigns. For new accounts, we design the structure, tracking, targeting, creative plan and landing page path from the ground up. For existing accounts, we start with an audit, fix measurement gaps, reduce waste, improve targeting, and run structured tests to lift conversion rate and ROI/ROAS. Optimisation is ongoing, not a one-time “tune-up”.

Do you provide Google Ads management and search advertising services?+

Yes. Nov8rs provides Google Ads management and search advertising services, including Google search ads built around high-intent keyword themes. We handle campaign setup, match type strategy, negative keywords, ad assets, audience layers, conversion tracking, and ongoing PPC management. You’ll see what’s being tested, what’s being optimised, and how changes impact cost per lead/sale and ROI/ROAS—not just surface-level metrics.

Can you run and optimise Google Performance Max campaigns?+

Yes. When Google Performance Max is the right fit, Nov8rs sets it up with clear conversion goals, clean tracking, strong creative inputs, and sensible audience signals. We monitor performance, search terms/insights, creative learnings, and budget pacing, then optimise based on what drives profitable outcomes. The aim is to keep performance explainable and controllable, rather than letting automation run without guardrails.

Do you manage Microsoft Advertising and Bing Ads?+

Yes. Nov8rs can act as your Bing Ads agency, managing Microsoft Advertising end-to-end. That includes Bing advertising account setup, keyword and audience targeting, tracking, and Bing Ads management and optimisation. If you want to advertise on Bing to capture additional search demand, we’ll align your Bing Ads strategy with your Google Ads management approach so reporting, budgets and learnings translate across platforms.

Can you manage LinkedIn Ads for B2B lead generation?+

Yes. As a LinkedIn Ads agency for B2B teams, Nov8rs supports targeting strategy (titles, seniority, industries, company lists), messaging and creative testing, and conversion tracking across LinkedIn lead gen forms and landing pages. If you’re seeking a B2B digital marketing agency USA-focused, we optimise LinkedIn lead generation towards lead quality and pipeline outcomes, not just volume.

Do you manage Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram advertising)?+

Yes. Nov8rs works as a Meta Ads agency, managing Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads for lead generation and customer acquisition. We handle Meta Ads management across setup, pixel/event configuration, audience testing, creative iteration, budget pacing, and optimisation. Where performance stalls, we’ll diagnose whether the issue is targeting, creative, offer, or the landing page experience—and recommend the next change accordingly.

How does your PPC management process work?+

Our PPC management services follow a repeatable cycle: goals and tracking setup → research and build → launch → test → optimise → report → scale. As your PPC agency, Nov8rs reviews queries/placements, bids, budgets, audiences and creatives, and runs structured A/B testing. We document what changed and why, so you can see how optimisation decisions connect back to performance—cost per lead, conversion rate and ROI/ROAS.

How do you reduce wasted ad spend?+

Wasted spend usually comes from weak intent, poor tracking, or misaligned landing pages. Nov8rs reduces waste through search term and placement audits, negative keyword strategy, tighter targeting, better conversion tracking, and smarter budget allocation. We also improve post-click performance with landing page and conversion path tweaks. This combination—campaign optimisation plus conversion optimisation—is how performance marketing services drive more value from the same budget.

Do you optimise landing pages and conversion paths for better ROI/ROAS?+

Yes. As a digital marketing agency USA-wide support partner (even if you found us while searching “marketing agencies near me”), Nov8rs helps improve landing pages and conversion paths that impact paid performance. We look at message match, friction, page speed, form design and trust signals, then prioritise changes you can implement quickly. Better conversion rates typically reduce cost per lead/sale and improve ROI/ROAS without increasing spend.

How does Nov8rs use AI in paid advertising?+

Nov8rs uses AI-powered advertising carefully—mainly to improve research, testing velocity and decision-making. That can include AI PPC management support for analysing search queries and creative performance, and AI ad optimisation ideas that a strategist validates before changes go live. We also monitor emerging advertising platforms and evolving AI search advertising opportunities (sometimes referred to as “ChatGPT Ads”) and advise when they become practical, measurable options for your goals.
