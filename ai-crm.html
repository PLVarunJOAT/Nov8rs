FAQ

Questions, answered.

Common questions about ai crm with Nov8rs (Innovators).

What is the Nov8rs AI CRM?+

It's our own CRM and automation platform with AI built in. It brings calls, texts, emails, chats and social messages into one inbox, then uses AI agents and workflows to reply, qualify, book appointments and request reviews automatically.

What is missed call text back?+

When a call goes unanswered, the system instantly sends the caller a text so the conversation continues. Our AI agent can then answer questions, qualify the lead and offer appointment times, so a missed call no longer means a lost customer.

Can AI agents make outbound calls?+

Yes. AI voice agents can call new leads, follow up on quotes and confirm appointments, using a script and tone you approve. Calls are logged in the CRM so your team can see every conversation.

Can the AI CRM request reviews automatically?+

Yes. Automated review requests go out after a job is marked complete, by text or email, with a direct link to your Google review page. Happy customers get a nudge and your reputation grows without manual chasing.

Will it work with my website and ads?+

Yes. Website forms, landing pages, ad lead forms, calendars and phone numbers all feed the same pipeline. That's how we track a lead from the first click to the booked job.

Is it a replacement for HubSpot or Zoho?+

For many service businesses, yes. It covers CRM, pipelines, inbox, booking, automation and reviews in one place, often at a lower cost than stitching several tools together. If you need to keep an existing CRM, we can integrate instead.

Do you set everything up for me?+

Yes. Nov8rs (Innovators) sets up your pipelines, inbox, calendars, workflows, AI agent prompts and integrations, then trains your team. We keep improving the automations as we see real lead data.

Can agencies resell the AI CRM?+

Yes. We offer a white label CRM option for agencies that want to provide their clients a branded CRM and automation platform. See our white-label page or build a partner quote.
