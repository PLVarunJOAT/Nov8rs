FAQ

Questions, answered.

Clear answers on how Nov8rs (Innovators) helps you get found, generate leads and scale what works.

What does Nov8rs do?+

Nov8rs helps businesses grow through digital strategy, SEO, paid advertising, web development, automation, AI and technology. We bring these areas together into a practical growth system focused on visibility, leads and measurable business outcomes.

Can Nov8rs help local businesses get more customers?+

Yes. We help local businesses improve their online visibility through local SEO, Google Business Profile optimisation, search visibility, paid advertising, websites and conversion-focused digital strategies designed to turn local searches into enquiries.

Do you work with trade and service businesses?+

Yes. Nov8rs works with businesses such as trades, home services, professional services and other customer-focused businesses that rely on local or regional enquiries. The strategy is built around how customers actually search, compare and make contact.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?+

SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search results. AEO focuses on making information easier for answer engines and AI systems to understand and use. GEO focuses on visibility within generative search and AI-driven discovery. Nov8rs can build these approaches into one broader search visibility strategy.

Can you help my business appear in Google and AI search results?+

Yes. Nov8rs works across traditional search and emerging AI-driven discovery. This can include technical SEO, content, local SEO, structured information, authority signals, AEO and GEO strategies depending on the business and its market.

Do you manage Google Ads?+

Yes. Nov8rs can manage Google Ads campaigns with a focus on qualified traffic, enquiries and measurable returns. Campaign structure, targeting, landing pages, tracking and ongoing optimisation are considered together rather than treating advertising as an isolated service.

Can you build or redesign my website?+

Yes. Nov8rs provides website design and development focused on performance, usability, conversion and search visibility. We can work on new websites, redesigns, landing pages and ongoing website improvements.

Can you improve my existing website without rebuilding it?+

Yes. A complete rebuild is not always necessary. We can identify issues affecting performance, visibility, user experience and conversions and recommend improvements based on what your existing website actually needs.

Do you provide marketing automation and AI solutions?+

Yes. Nov8rs can help businesses use automation and AI to reduce repetitive work, improve lead handling, streamline processes and create more efficient marketing and business systems.

How do I know which services my business actually needs?+

You do not need to figure that out before speaking with us. We look at your current visibility, website, traffic, lead generation, advertising and business objectives, then identify the areas that can have the greatest practical impact.

How long does it take to see results from SEO and digital marketing?+

It depends on the service, starting position, competition and market. Paid advertising can generate traffic much faster, while SEO and organic visibility generally require consistent optimisation and time to build. We focus on measurable progress rather than promising unrealistic timelines.
